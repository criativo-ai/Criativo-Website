DigiVal

Digital Exam Evaluation Software for Schools and Colleges

DigiVal is a digital exam evaluation software built for schools, colleges, and universities that need faster results, fewer disputes, and complete accountability. It combines answer script digitization, remote examiner evaluation, and paperless grading to replace manual correction with a secure and scalable system.

What Digital Exam Evaluation Means

Digital exam evaluation converts physical answer scripts into secure digital files, assigns scripts to examiners online, supports on-screen marking, automates totalling, and tracks progress in real time. DigiVal functions as a complete evaluation system that removes paper movement, reduces errors, and improves transparency.

Why Manual Exam Evaluation Creates Risk:
  • Scripts can be misplaced or delayed
  • Totals can be miscalculated
  • Results are published late
  • External examiner coordination becomes expensive
  • Audit checks require manual verification across scattered records
  • Risks impact credibility, admissions cycles, and institutional efficiency
  • DigiVal digitizes the workflow end-to-end to remove these vulnerabilities
How DigiVal Works
  • Answer booklets are scanned using standard scanners
  • Scripts are securely uploaded to the platform
  • Controllers allocate scripts digitally
  • Examiners evaluate on screen
  • Totals are calculated automatically
  • Leadership tracks evaluation progress in real time
  • Results are published faster with a complete audit trail
Proven at Scale
  • 388,905 scripts evaluated
  • 53 campuses onboarded
  • 38,716 hours saved
  • Average grading time reduced from 15 minutes to 9 minutes per script
  • 6 minutes saved per script

Deployment mix includes 35 degree colleges, 12 schools, and 5 postgraduate campuses. Degree colleges represent the majority of current deployments.

For Degree and Engineering Institutions

DigiVal reduces script transport, storage overhead, and external examiner travel dependency. It enables structured digital allocation, faster completion, and real-time oversight for Controllers of Examinations.

Engineering institutions benefit from scalable evaluation infrastructure designed for high script volumes.

For Schools (Classes 8–12)

DigiVal enables faster results, reduced disputes, and improved accuracy through automated totalling. Teachers retain full academic control while eliminating paperwork.

Where required, leadership can provide structured visibility into evaluated scripts, strengthening parent trust.

Flexible by Design

DigiVal adapts to university valuation rules, internal assessments, board formats, and subject-specific marking structures. Institutions retain their academic process while removing inefficiency.

Key Controls

  • Scripts remain securely stored
  • Audit readiness is built into the system

Frequently Asked Questions

Will teachers resist digital evaluation?

DigiVal preserves marking methodology. It removes paperwork and manual totalling without changing academic judgement.

Can DigiVal support external valuation?

Yes. Remote examiner evaluation and structured allocation are core capabilities.

What if internet connectivity is unreliable?

Scripts can be scanned and uploaded in batches. Evaluation progress remains stable and traceable.

Can institutions start with a pilot?

Yes. DigiVal supports phased adoption across departments and exam cycles.
