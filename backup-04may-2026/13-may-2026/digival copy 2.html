What Digital Exam Evaluation Means

Digital exam evaluation converts physical answer scripts into secure digital files, assigns scripts to examiners online, supports on‑screen marking, automates totalling, and tracks progress in real time.

DigiVal functions as a complete evaluation system that removes paper movement, reduces errors, and improves transparency.
