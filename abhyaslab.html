Abhyas Lab: AI-Assisted Coding Practice Platform

Abhyas Lab is Criativo’s AI-assisted coding practice platform that helps students build programming skills through structured practice and tracked progress across coding fundamentals and problem-solving.

What Abhyas Lab Enables

  • Structured coding practice for skill-building
  • Progress tracking across topics and difficulty levels
  • Guided improvement through repeated practice cycles
  • Clear visibility into learner readiness for coding rounds
