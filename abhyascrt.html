Abhyas CRT : Placement Assistance Platform

Abhyas CRT is Criativo’s placement assistance platform that helps students prepare for campus recruitment through aptitude practice, progress tracking, and structured improvement plans aligned to hiring test patterns.

What Abhyas CRT Enables

  • A structured aptitude practice pathway for placements
  • Topic-level performance tracking across aptitude areas
  • Personalized practice plans based on weak areas
  • Cohort readiness visibility for placement teams
